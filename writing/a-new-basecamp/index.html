Updates / November 1, 2026

A new basecamp.

A fresh home for the things I make, the things I'm learning, and the space in between.

Dzinh Pham · 2 min read

I'm rebuilding this site as both a portfolio and a personal hub. The projects are still here, but I want to make room for more than finished work.

That means writing, learning notes, and occasional updates. Some things might grow into longer posts. Others might stay short: an idea worth keeping, something that finally clicked, or a small update on what I'm working on.

A little inspiration from bouldering

I wanted bouldering to be part of the feel of this space, too. The design borrows from climbing holds, rock, and the lines of a route. It's a thread through the site, alongside the code and projects.

I like the idea of a personal site as a basecamp: somewhere to gather what matters, take a look around, and decide what comes next.

Starting with a little space

For now, this is the first note. The notebook will grow as I have things to share. There doesn't need to be a fixed schedule or a finished answer for every thought.

If you're new here, take a look at the projects, read a little about me, or say hello. I'm glad you're here.

One move at a time.
