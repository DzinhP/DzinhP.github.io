How to climb

Keyboard · Focus the wall, use arrows to select, then Space or Enter to move. R rests on a jug. Tab leaves the wall.

Touch & mouse · Pick a hold, then Make the move. Rest when you reach a J hold.

Read ahead · Reachable shortcuts cost extra. Mint holds and moves beyond your reach cost 8 endurance without advancing. Run out before the summit and you’re back on the mat.
